ACM walkthrough
  1. Copy the paper DOI or ACM article URL.
  2. Paste it above and click Load references.
  3. Select the paper and choose a destination folder.
  4. Start the download. Author copies and OA sources are tried automatically.
  5. If blocked, expand Attempt details. Paste a verified alternate PDF link beneath the reference or use the article's normal browser download.

Article access and publisher automation rules determine availability. A valid DOI does not guarantee a downloadable PDF.
